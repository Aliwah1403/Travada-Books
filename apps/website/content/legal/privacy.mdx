This is placeholder copy. It sets out the sections our real Privacy Policy will
cover, so you know what's coming — it isn't the final wording and shouldn't be
relied on as one.

## Definitions

"We", "us" and "Travada Books" mean Travada Systems, the business behind this
product. "You" means the person or business using it.

## What we collect

Account details, the invoices, customers and transactions you add or import,
files you upload (statements, receipts), and product analytics on how the
app is used (we use PostHog for this). If you connect an email inbox, see the
Google and Microsoft sections below for what that adds.

## How we use it

To run the product for you — generating invoices, importing statements,
matching receipts — and to understand how the product is used so we can
improve it. To read statements and receipts, categorise transactions, and
match documents to the transaction they belong to, we send the relevant
files and transaction details to AI service providers (Google, OpenAI and
Mistral), who process them on our behalf.

## Google user data

If you connect a Gmail account, Travada Books requests read-only access
(`gmail.readonly`) so it can find PDF receipts and invoices sent to you. It
cannot send email as you or change anything in your inbox, and you can
disconnect it at any time. PDFs found this way are processed the same way as
any other receipt — read and matched to a transaction, as described above.
Travada Books' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
<a href="https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy">Google API Services User Data Policy</a>, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Microsoft user data

If you connect an Outlook account, Travada Books requests read-only access
(`Mail.Read`) for the same purpose — finding PDF receipts and invoices sent
to you. It cannot send email as you, and you can disconnect it at any time.

## Legal basis

We process personal data under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019. The final
policy will set out, section by section, which legal basis applies to each
use described above.

## Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. A specific
retention schedule for closed accounts will be added here.

## Transfers

Some of the services we rely on to run Travada Books may process data outside
Kenya. The final policy will list these and the safeguards that apply.

## Disclosure

We don't sell your data. We share it only with the service providers that
help us run the product (for example, hosting, email delivery, and the AI
providers described above), and where the law requires it.

## Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data, including access controls and
encryption in transit. No system is completely secure, and we'll say more
about our specific measures in the final policy.

## Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you can ask to access, correct or
delete your personal data. The final policy will explain how to do that.
