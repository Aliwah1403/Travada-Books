Settings had outgrown its narrow column. Forms were squeezed into a strip down the middle of the screen, and every new section made the sidebar longer.

## Tabs across the top

Settings and Account both use a wider, tabbed layout now. Settings is split into General, Team, Categories, Inbox and Integrations, each one tab away. Forms use the full width of the page instead of a thin column, so there's less scrolling to find the field you want.

## Integrations as cards

The Integrations tab is now a gallery. Each integration gets a card that shows whether it's connected, with a switch to turn it on and a confirmation before anything is disconnected.

Open a connected card for its detail page: the accounts you've connected, **Sync now**, **Reconnect** and **Remove** for each one, and a plain-language list of what Travada Books can and can't access.

Integrations that aren't ready yet sit in the same gallery, clearly labelled as coming soon, so you can see what's on the way.
