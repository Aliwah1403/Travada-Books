Travada Books is now in public beta. Anyone can sign up, free, with no card needed.

We started building it because we were tired of doing our own books by hand: rebuilding the same invoices every month, chasing payments, and sorting statements into spreadsheets. It now runs our books, and a small group of early users have been running theirs on it too. Today we're opening it to everyone.

## What you can do today

- **Invoices that send themselves.** Schedule an invoice, make it recurring, and let reminders follow up when it's late. [See invoicing](/invoicing).
- **Quotes that become invoices.** Your customer accepts from a link, and the invoice is drafted for you. [See quotes](/quotes).
- **Payments, in full or in parts.** Record what came in and the balance and status keep themselves straight. [See payments](/payments).
- **Statements, sorted.** Upload a bank or mobile money statement, such as M-Pesa, as a CSV or PDF, and every transaction comes in categorised. [See statement import](/statement-import).
- **Receipts that find you.** Connect Gmail or Outlook, or forward receipts by email, and Travada Books matches them to the right transaction. [See the inbox](/inbox).
- **Your numbers in one place.** A dashboard for revenue, spending, cash flow and who owes you, plus a Vault for your documents and room for your team.

## What beta means

Travada Books is free while it's in beta. Some things will change as we learn from how you use it, and we'll write up every change here in Updates.

We read everything you send us. If something takes more steps than it should, or you need something it doesn't do yet, [tell us what to build next](/contact?topic=feature).
