Travada Books is now open for beta. It brings the routine around invoicing and bookkeeping into one place for freelancers and small businesses in Kenya.

## Invoices can run on a schedule

Create an invoice once and choose whether it repeats weekly, fortnightly, monthly, quarterly, or yearly. You can preview upcoming send dates, schedule delivery, and configure reminders for overdue invoices.

Quotes can be shared with a customer through a public link. When a quote is accepted, it can become an invoice without retyping the work.

[Explore invoicing](/invoicing).

## Bring the records you already have

Statement import accepts CSV and PDF files from banks and mobile money providers such as M-Pesa. Travada Books detects common date, description, debit, credit, and amount columns, then brings the transactions into a reviewable list.

Categories and bulk actions help clear a backlog without editing every transaction separately.

[See statement import](/statement-import).

## Receipts can arrive from email

Gmail and Outlook connections are available now. Travada Books uses read-only access to find PDF receipts and supplier invoices, keep them in the Vault, and suggest the transaction they belong to.

M-Pesa payments, Stripe payments, and WhatsApp delivery are on the integration roadmap and remain labelled as coming soon until they are ready.

[See the integration roadmap](/integrations).

## What beta means

Travada Books is free during beta. We are using this period to learn from real business workflows, improve imports, and make the repetitive parts of running the books more dependable.

If something in your workflow still takes too many steps, email [info@travadabooks.com](mailto:info@travadabooks.com).
