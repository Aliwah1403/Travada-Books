Invoice links get buried in email threads and chats, and then the customer asks you to send it again. The customer portal gives each customer one link that always has everything.

## What your customer sees

The portal opens with your logo and business name, then the balance due, how many invoices are overdue and a link to the oldest one. Below that sit the total invoiced, the total paid and the number of invoices.

Invoices are split into Outstanding and Paid tabs. Part-paid invoices show what's left to pay, and any invoice opens in full or downloads as a PDF. Quotes waiting for an answer are listed so the customer can open one and accept it, and every statement you've generated for them is there too.

It's laid out for a phone, because that's where most customers will open it.

## You stay in control

- The portal is off until you switch it on for a customer, from their page in Travada Books.
- Copy the link or share it on WhatsApp.
- There's no login. The private link is the key.
- If it ever reaches the wrong person, regenerate it and the old link stops working straight away.
