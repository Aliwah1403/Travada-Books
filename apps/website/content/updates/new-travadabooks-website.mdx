Travada Books has a new home at travadabooks.com. It's where we explain what the product does, show how it works, and keep a record of what changes.

## A page for each part of the product

Invoicing, quotes, payments, the customer portal, statement import and Inbox each have their own page now. Each one shows what the feature does day to day, with answers to common questions.

The [integrations page](/integrations) shows what connects today and what's still on the way. Anything that isn't ready is labelled as coming soon.

## A help centre

[Guides](/guides) walk through getting set up and the routine jobs, step by step, with longer reads on running the books for a business in Kenya.

## This changelog

Every product update now gets written up here, newest first. Filter by product or company news, or share a link to a single update.
