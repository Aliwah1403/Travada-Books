The forwarding address works, but it still asks you to remember to forward. Now you can connect the mailbox your receipts already arrive in and let Travada Books collect them.

## Gmail and Outlook

Go to Settings → Integrations and connect a Gmail or Outlook account. Gmail came first, and Outlook followed a day later with the same setup.

## What it picks up

Travada Books looks for PDF attachments, such as receipts and supplier invoices, and brings them into your Inbox. From there they're read, matched to a transaction and filed in the Vault exactly like a forwarded receipt. Anything from a sender on your blocklist is skipped.

Connected accounts are checked every six hours. If you're waiting on a receipt, open the account's detail page and press **Sync now**.

## Your mailbox stays yours

- Access is read-only. Travada Books can't send, delete or change your email.
- It only brings in PDF attachments, not your messages.
- Sign-in tokens are encrypted before they're stored.
- Disconnect whenever you like from the same page.
