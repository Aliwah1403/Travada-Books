A quote tells a customer what a job will cost before you start. They can accept or decline it online, and an accepted quote becomes a draft invoice for you. Quotes live under **Quotes** in the sidebar, right below **Invoices**.

## Fill in the quote

1. Select **Quotes**, then **+ New Quote**.
2. Under **Prepared For**, search for your customer. If they're new, type their name and choose **Create "…"**.
3. Check the details at the top:
   - **Quote #** fills in with your next number once you've picked a customer. You can type your own, but each number can only be used once in your business.
   - **Currency** starts as your base currency. Change it if you're quoting in another one, such as USD or AED.
   - **Issue Date** and **Valid Until**. Valid Until is how long your offer stands. If you've set a default validity period, it fills itself in.
4. Add extra details like a project name or reference with **Add field**. See [Invoice settings and custom fields](/guides/invoice-settings-and-custom-fields).
5. Add your line items, each with a **Description**, **Qty** and **Rate**. Select **Add line item** for more.
6. Under **Tax & Discounts**, add a **Discount** (a percentage or a fixed amount) and a **VAT / Tax %** if they apply.
7. Add **Notes** for your terms, what's included, or anything else the customer should know.

The preview on the right shows the quote as your customer will see it.

## Send it or save it

The main button at the top right does one of two things. Use the arrow beside it to choose:

- **Send Quote** saves the quote and emails it to your customer now.
- **Save as Draft** saves it without sending.

The email has a **Review Quote** button that opens the quote online, where your customer can accept or decline it. See [Quote responses and invoices](/guides/quote-responses-and-invoices).

## Send a draft

Open the draft and select **Send Quote**. Then choose how you're sending it:

- **Email** sends it to the customer's email address.
- **WhatsApp** opens WhatsApp with the quote link ready to send.
- **Copy link** copies the link so you can paste it anywhere.
- **Download PDF** saves the PDF so you can send it yourself.
- **Mark as sent** is for when you've already sent it another way.

Whichever you choose, the quote's status changes from **Draft** to **Sent**. A draft's link won't open for your customer until then.

## Quote settings

Select **Quote Settings** at the top of the quote form to set defaults for every new quote:

- **Default Validity Period**: 7, 14, 30, 60 or 90 days. **Valid Until** then fills itself in from the issue date.
- **Quote Number**: a prefix, such as `QUO-`, and 3, 4 or 5 digits. This is locked once you've created your first quote.
- **Default Note** and **Default Custom Fields**, filled in on every new quote.
- **Attach PDF**, to include a PDF copy in the quote email.
- **Send Copy**, to CC or BCC someone on quote emails.

Your changes are saved when you close the panel.

## Work with a sent quote

At the top of any quote you can select **Download PDF** or **Copy Link**. Under the **•••** menu:

- **Duplicate** makes a new draft copy with the next quote number. It keeps the original dates, so update **Issue Date** and **Valid Until** before you send it.
- **Delete** removes the quote for good.

You can edit a quote while it's a draft, using the pencil icon at the top.

Under **Activity**, you can see when the quote was created, when and how it was sent, when your customer first viewed it, and whether it's still **Awaiting response**. Use **Internal note** for private notes your customer never sees.
