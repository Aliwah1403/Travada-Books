Every invoice starts from **Invoices** in the sidebar. You fill in a form on the left and watch the finished invoice take shape in the preview on the right.

## Fill in the invoice

1. Select **Invoices**, then **+ New Invoice**.
2. Under **Bill To**, search for your customer. If they're new, type their name and choose **Create "…"** to add them without leaving the invoice.
3. Check the details at the top:
   - **Invoice #** is filled in with your next number. You can type your own.
   - **Currency** starts as your base currency. Change it if you're billing in another one.
   - **Issue Date** and **Due Date**. If you've set payment terms, the due date fills itself in from the issue date.
4. Add your line items. Each one needs a **Description**, a **Qty** and a **Rate**. Select **Add line item** for another line, or the bin icon to remove one.
5. Under **Tax & Discounts**, add a **Discount** (a percentage or a fixed amount) and a **VAT / Tax %** if they apply. The totals update as you type.
6. Add **Payment Details** so your customer knows how to pay you, and any **Notes**. Both are optional.

## Send it, save it or schedule it

The main button at the top right does one of three things. Use the arrow beside it to choose:

- **Create + Send** saves the invoice and emails it to your customer now.
- **Create Invoice** saves it as a draft. Nothing goes to your customer yet.
- **Schedule Send** saves it and sends it on the date you pick.

The same menu has **Recurring**, for invoices that repeat. See [Set up a recurring invoice](/guides/set-up-a-recurring-invoice).

## Send a draft

When you open a draft, you'll see **Send Invoice** at the top. Select it and choose how you're sending it:

1. **Email** sends it to the customer's email address.
2. **WhatsApp** opens WhatsApp with the invoice link ready to send.
3. **Copy link** copies the invoice link so you can paste it anywhere.
4. **Download PDF** saves the PDF so you can send it yourself.
5. **Mark as sent** is for when you've already sent it another way.

Whichever you choose, the invoice is marked as sent and its status changes from **Draft**.

## What your customer sees

Your customer opens a clean web page with your logo, their details, the line items and the total, and can download a PDF from there. They don't need an account.

> **Good to know:** You can edit an invoice while it's a draft. Once it's sent, it keeps the business and customer details it was sent with, so the invoice your customer has always matches yours. To bill for something similar again, open the invoice, select the **•••** menu and choose **Duplicate**.

## If the invoice number is taken

Invoice numbers must be unique for each customer. If you type a number you've already used for that customer, the **Invoice #** field turns red and tells you. Pick another number and try again.

## Change what every invoice looks like

Select **Invoice Settings** at the top of the form to set your logo, how invoice numbers are formatted, your payment terms, automatic reminders, and a default note and payment details. They're saved for every new invoice.
