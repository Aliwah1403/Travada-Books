Your data is yours. You can take a full copy of it at any time, and if you leave, Travada Books emails you an export before anything is deleted.

## Export all your data

Only owners can export.

1. Open the menu under your business name at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **General**.
2. Under **Export all data**, select **Export all data**.
3. A message confirms the email address the export will go to.

Travada Books bundles your data into a ZIP file and emails you a download link when it's ready. Exports usually take a few minutes. While one is running, the button shows **Export in progress…**, and you can't start another.

The download link stays valid for **7 days**. After that, start a new export.

### What's included

- your customers, invoices, quotes, statements and transactions, as spreadsheet (CSV) files
- your files: transaction attachments, Inbox documents and everything in your Vault
- a list of your team members

Very large exports are split into several ZIP files, each with its own link.

Once an export has finished, **Last export** shows when it was made. If it failed, try again, or [contact us](/contact) if it keeps happening.

## Delete your organisation

Only owners can delete an organisation. It deletes everything in it, for everyone.

1. Open the menu under your business name and choose **Team**.
2. Under **Delete organisation**, select **Delete organisation**.
3. Read the warning, type **DELETE** in the box, and select **Delete organisation**.

What happens next:

- All members lose access straight away.
- Recurring invoices stop.
- A full export is emailed to you first, with a download link valid for **30 days**.
- Then the organisation and all its data are permanently deleted: invoices, quotes, customers, transactions and files.

You're taken to set up a new organisation. If you belong to others, you can switch to them from the sidebar.

This can't be undone. If you just want to stop someone's access, [remove them from your team](/guides/invite-your-team) instead.

## Delete your account

1. Select your avatar at the top right and choose **Security**.
2. Under **Delete account**, select **Delete account**.
3. Type **DELETE** in the box and select **Delete account**.

What happens to your organisations depends on who else is in them:

- **Organisations where you're the only member** are deleted too. Each one is exported and emailed to you first, with a download link valid for **30 days**.
- **Organisations with other members** aren't affected. You're simply removed, and everyone else keeps their access and data.

You're signed out straight away. This can't be undone.

> **Good to know:** If you're the only owner of an organisation that has other members, make someone else an owner before you delete your account, so the team can still manage it. See [Invite your team](/guides/invite-your-team).
