Once you've [sent a quote](/guides/create-and-send-a-quote), your customer answers it online. You don't have to chase a signature or retype anything. An accepted quote turns into a draft invoice for you.

## What your customer sees

The quote link opens a web page with the full quote: your details, the line items, totals, notes and the **Valid until** date. They can select **Download PDF** or **Copy Link**, and they don't need an account.

At the bottom are two buttons:

- **Accept Quote** accepts it in one click.
- **Decline** opens a short form where they can add a **Reason**, which is optional, before selecting **Decline Quote**.

Either way, they see a confirmation page. If they accept, it tells them you'll send the invoice shortly.

## When a customer accepts

The quote's status changes to **Accepted**, and Travada Books creates a draft invoice from it straight away. The invoice has:

- the same customer, currency, line items, discount, tax and total,
- the same custom fields,
- your next invoice number, with today as the issue date,
- a due date worked out from your **Payment Terms**, if you've set them, and
- the quote's notes, or your **Default Note** if the quote had none, plus your **Default Payment Details**.

The invoice stays a draft. Nothing is sent to your customer until you send it.

On the quote, a green banner says **Invoice INV-… was created from this quote.** Select **View Invoice** to open it. Check it over, make any changes, then send it as usual. See [Create and send an invoice](/guides/create-and-send-an-invoice).

An accepted quote is locked, so it can't be edited or accepted twice.

## When a customer declines

The status changes to **Declined**. The quote shows a **Quote declined** banner, with their reason if they gave one. The reason is also listed under **Activity**.

If you want to try again:

1. Open the quote and select the pencil icon to change it, for example to adjust the price or the scope.
2. Select **Save Changes**. The quote goes back to **Draft**.
3. Select **Send Quote** to send the revised version.

If nothing needs to change, select **Resend Quote** and choose how to send it, to send it again as it is. **Activity** shows when it was resent.

## Get told when a customer answers

You can get an email, an in-app notification or both when a quote is accepted or declined. The email for an accepted quote has a **Review Draft Invoice** button that takes you straight to the new invoice. Emails go to the business email in your settings.

Choose which alerts you get under **Quote accepted** and **Quote declined** in your [notification settings](/guides/notification-settings).

## Quotes that run out of time

If a customer hasn't answered when the **Valid Until** date passes, the quote becomes **Expired**. Your customer's link then says the quote has expired, and the **Accept Quote** and **Decline** buttons disappear.

An expired quote can't be edited or resent. To make a fresh offer, open it, select the **•••** menu and choose **Duplicate**. Update the **Issue Date** and **Valid Until** on the copy, then send it.

> **Good to know:** To give customers longer to decide, set a **Default Validity Period** in **Quote Settings**, or pick a later **Valid Until** date before you send.
