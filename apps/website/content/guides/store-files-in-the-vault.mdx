The **Vault** is where your business documents live: contracts, licences, tax papers, receipts and anything else you need to keep. Files you add elsewhere in Travada Books end up here too, so you don't have to hunt for them.

## What's already in your Vault

Alongside files you upload yourself, the Vault collects:

- files that arrive in your **Inbox**
- receipts and files attached to transactions
- PDFs of invoices you send, filed in the **Invoices** folder
- PDFs of quotes and statements you send

Each document shows where it came from, such as **Upload**, **Inbox**, **Transaction**, **Invoice**, **Quote** or **Statement**.

## Upload a file

1. Select **Vault** in the sidebar.
2. Select **Upload**, or drag files onto the page.

You can upload images, PDFs, Word, Excel and PowerPoint files, and CSVs. If you're inside a folder, the file goes into that folder.

Travada Books reads each file and gives it a clear title, a one-line description and a few tags, so it's easier to find later. You'll see a small spinner on the document while this happens.

## Folders

Every Vault starts with six folders: **Contracts**, **Tax & Compliance**, **Receipts**, **Invoices**, **Licenses** and **Bank Documents**.

- **Create a folder:** select **New** at the end of the folder row, enter a **Folder name**, and select **Create**. Create one while you're inside another folder to nest it.
- **Move a file:** open the document's **⋮** menu, choose **Move to folder**, and pick a folder. Choose **Remove from folder** to take it out.
- **Delete a folder:** hover over it and select the small **×**. The files inside aren't deleted, they just leave the folder. The six starting folders can't be deleted.

Use the path at the top, starting with **All**, to go back up.

## Find a document

Type in **Search documents…** and press Enter. A single word searches names, titles and the text of your documents.

You can also search in plain words, such as "contracts from last year". Travada Books works out the date range and search term for you.

To filter by hand, select the filter icon in the search box and choose **Date** (when the document was added) or **Tags**. Switch between grid and list view with the buttons on the right.

## Open and edit a document

Select a document to preview it. From here you can:

- change its **Folder**
- edit the **Description**, or select **Auto-fill** to have Travada Books write it again
- add or remove **Tags** (press Enter or a comma after each one)
- see **Related documents**

## Share a document by link

1. Open the document's **⋮** menu and choose **Copy share link**.
2. The link is copied. Paste it into an email or message.

Anyone with the link can download the file, without an account. The link works for 30 days, and the page shows the date it expires.

## Download, rename or delete

From a document's **⋮** menu:

- **Download** saves a copy.
- **Rename** changes its name.
- **Extract to transaction** reads a receipt or invoice (images and PDFs only) and opens a new transaction with the details filled in, ready for you to check and save.
- **Delete** removes the document and its file.

To act on several documents at once, tick them. A bar appears at the bottom where you can **Download** them as one ZIP file, **Move to folder**, or **Delete** them.

> **Good to know:** Deleting a document can't be undone. If you might need it later, download a copy first.
