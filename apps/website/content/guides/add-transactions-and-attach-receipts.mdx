Most transactions arrive through a [statement import](/guides/import-a-bank-or-mpesa-statement). For anything that isn't on a statement, such as a cash sale or a one-off expense, add it by hand.

## Add a transaction

1. Select **Transactions** in the sidebar, then **+ New Transaction**.
2. Choose **Income** or **Expense**.
3. Enter the amount and pick the currency beside it.
4. Add a **Description**, and who you were paid by (**Received from**) or who you paid (**Paid to**).
5. Set the date. The status starts as **Completed**. Change it to **Pending** if the money hasn't cleared yet.
6. Fill in any of the other details you need:
   - **Category** and **Payment mode**.
   - **Reference no.**, such as a transaction code or receipt number.
   - **Tax rate (%)**, **Tax amount** and **Tax type**.
   - **Recurring**, for something that repeats, such as rent. Then choose how often.
   - **Exclude from P&L**, for transfers between your own accounts.
   - **Note**.
7. Select **Add transaction**.

Travada Books suggests a category if you didn't choose one. See [Categorise transactions](/guides/categorise-transactions).

## Link income to an invoice

For income, use **Link to invoice** to pick the open invoice this money was for. You can search by invoice number or customer. Tick **Mark invoice as paid when saving** to close the invoice at the same time.

## Attach a receipt

1. Open the transaction, or start a new one.
2. Under **Attachments**, select **Click to upload receipt or document**, or drag the file onto that area. You can add PDF, PNG or JPG files up to 10 MB each, and more than one per transaction.
3. Select **Add transaction**, or **Save changes** if you're editing.

Before you save, you can select **Extract** beside a file you've just added, and Travada Books reads the receipt and fills in the amount, date, who was paid and more. Check the details before you save.

If a transaction has no tax details yet, Travada Books reads the attached receipt and fills in the tax amount, rate and type if it finds them.

> **Good to know:** You can also drop receipt images or PDFs straight onto the Transactions page. Travada Books uploads them and matches each one to the right transaction for you. See [Match receipts to transactions](/guides/match-receipts-to-transactions).

## See what's attached

Transactions with a file show a paperclip in the **Attachment** column. Open the transaction to see each file's name and size under **Attachments**. To remove one, select its bin icon, then **Save changes**.

Attached files are also kept in your Vault. See [Store files in the Vault](/guides/store-files-in-the-vault).

## Edit or delete a transaction

Select a transaction to open it, make your changes and select **Save changes**. You can also choose **Edit** from the **•••** menu on its row.

To delete one, choose **Delete** from the **•••** menu and confirm. This can't be undone. To delete several, tick them and select **Delete** in the bar at the bottom.

## Invoice payments

When you [record a payment](/guides/record-a-payment) on an invoice, Travada Books adds it to your transactions as income. It's named "Invoice" followed by the invoice number, dated the day you were paid, and put in the **Sales Revenue** category. The **Invoice** column links back to the invoice. If you delete the payment from the invoice, its transaction is removed too.
