Most receipts and supplier invoices already arrive by email. Connect your Gmail or Outlook mailbox and Travada Books collects the PDF attachments into your **Inbox**, reads them, and suggests which transaction each one belongs to.

## Connect your mailbox

1. Open the menu under your business name at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **Integrations**.
2. Find **Gmail** or **Outlook**. Turn on its switch, or open the card and select **Connect Gmail** or **Connect Outlook**.
3. Sign in to your Google or Microsoft account and allow access. Travada Books asks to read your messages and attachments, to find receipts and invoices, and to see your basic account details, to show which mailbox is connected.
4. You're brought back to your **Inbox**, and a message confirms the connection.

The card now shows **Connected as** and your email address.

## What gets collected

On the first sync, Travada Books looks back over the last 30 days of email for messages with PDF attachments. After that it checks for new ones regularly on its own.

Each PDF becomes an item in your **Inbox**. Travada Books reads the amount, currency, invoice number and sender, and saves a copy of the file to your **Vault**, so your documents stay in one place.

## Confirm the matches

When Travada Books finds a transaction that fits a receipt, the item shows a **Suggested match**.

1. Open the item in your **Inbox** and check the suggestion against the receipt.
2. Select **Confirm** to attach the receipt to that transaction, or **Decline** if it's wrong.

Travada Books learns from what you confirm, so matches from the same supplier get better over time.

## Manage the connection

Open **Integrations**, then the **Gmail** or **Outlook** card:

- **Sync now** checks for new email straight away.
- **Disconnect** stops collecting from that mailbox.
- If the connection stops working, the account shows as **Disconnected**. Select **Reconnect** and sign in again.

> **Good to know:** Only PDF attachments are collected, not the emails themselves. If a supplier sends newsletters or other noise you don't want, block them in **Settings**, then **Inbox**, under **Blocked senders**. And if you'd rather not connect a mailbox at all, every organisation has its own inbox email address (also under **Settings**, then **Inbox**). Forward receipts there and they land in your Inbox the same way.
